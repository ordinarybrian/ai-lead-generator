## Services and positioning

- Tagline/positioning: "Stop Renting Your Leads. Own the Machine That Makes It." — positions itself as building a full marketing "system" (foundational website/GBP/SEO, then Local Service Ads, Google Ads, CRM integration) rather than a one-off lead source.
- Core services: web design (custom builds, not templates), local SEO, Google Business Profile/citations work, Google Local Service Ads management, Google Ads campaigns, CRM integration, print marketing, audits/consultations.
- Claims "140+ local businesses across the country" as clients (site homepage) and, per the site's own About page, "8 years" of operation, 400+ contractor websites built, and roughly 150 monthly retainer clients. These are the company's own self-reported figures, not independently verified.
- Client base is explicitly nationwide, not just North Idaho — the CDA/Hayden office is described as the home base, with a dedicated /coeur-dalene/ landing page targeting "Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls, Hayden, and across North Idaho" specifically, alongside geo-targeted service pages for many other markets/trades (e.g. state-specific contractor-license-requirement articles).
- No long-term contracts; described as month-to-month across their offerings.

## Hosting and maintenance — critical fit finding

- **Adapt Digital Solutions already sells its own WordPress hosting + maintenance bundle in-house**, via a dedicated page: https://adaptdigitalsolutions.com/website-maintenance/ (retrieved 2026-08-10).
- Per that page: hosting is bundled into a single flat monthly rate ("Hosting is included in the flat monthly rate. Your site runs on our managed infrastructure — fast, secure, and monitored... no need for a separate hosting account"). Included: WordPress core/plugin updates, security monitoring, daily backups, uptime monitoring, monthly contact-form testing, break-fix support. Month-to-month, no contract.
- A general marketing/SEO summary of the same offering (via a broader site-wide search snippet, not independently re-verified line by line) described the price as roughly $50/month, though the maintenance page itself does not display a dollar figure directly (it says only "less than the cost of a single lunch out").
- **This contradicts the existing CSV `fit_reason`** ("no hosting or maintenance plan advertised"). That was evidently true of whatever page(s) were checked at `last_verified: 2026-08-08`, but the dedicated maintenance page was found this pass and clearly is live. This matters a great deal for outreach angle: a straightforward "send me your hosting/maintenance clients" referral pitch would now compete directly with an existing Adapt Digital revenue line, not fill a gap. See final report for a suggested reframe (white-label/backend angle, per PROJECT.md's standing white-label option) — no outreach was drafted as part of this task.

## Why this is a plausible fit (with a caveat)

- Matches PROJECT.md's "small marketing agency" target category: Coeur d'Alene/North Idaho-based, small team, web design + SEO/PPC services, explicitly serving contractors and local service businesses.
- However, unlike the ideal target profile ("hosting isn't prominently offered"), Adapt Digital Solutions has a live, actively marketed hosting+maintenance product of its own. This doesn't disqualify them, but it changes the likely angle from "take hosting off your plate" (which competes with them) toward a white-label/backend-infrastructure conversation, or simply relationship-building for edge cases (clients who want their own hosting account, non-WordPress needs, overflow capacity, etc.).
