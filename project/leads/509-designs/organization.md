# 509 Designs

## Organization

- Organization: 509 Designs (sole proprietorship / one-person shop, run largely by its founder)
- Website: https://509designs.com/
- Organization type: Local Spokane web design & SEO shop — "professional SEO services for local Spokane businesses, without the professional price tag."
- Based in Spokane, WA; started by Kevin Raverty roughly 4 years before this research (per search-summary sourcing, not independently dated on the site).

## Services and positioning

- Core services: WordPress and Squarespace website builds, local SEO, photography/videography (including aerial/drone work for real estate), logo/graphic design, and social media/Google Business Profile management.
- Positioning is "full-service" for small/new local businesses: "we help our customers with anything and everything that has to do with online marketing," emphasized as more affordable than larger competitors.
- No hosting or website-maintenance-plan offering found anywhere on the site (homepage or about page) — services stop at build/design/SEO/marketing, not ongoing infrastructure.

## Notable past work

- A named case study: took a Spokane motorcycle repair shop's website and local SEO to "#1 in the local map results" within weeks — this happens to line up with the founder's own personal interest in motorcycles (see people file).
- Also references past work for real estate clients (consistent with the aerial/drone photography service line).
- A dedicated /portfolio/ page returned a 404 on 2026-08-08 — could not review a fuller project list directly.

## Why this is a good fit

- Small, local, single-operator shop explicitly targeting small businesses with an affordability/accessibility pitch — a strong match for PROJECT.md's "freelance web/graphic designers" and "small marketing agencies" categories, and no existing hosting offering to create channel conflict.
- The business is young and growing (founder learned the trade ~4 years ago via a personal 3D-printing YouTube channel before turning it into a business) — plausibly still building out its vendor/partner relationships rather than locked into an existing hosting arrangement.
