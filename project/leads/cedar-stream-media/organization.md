## Hosting posture — competing product (confirmed)

- Cedar Stream Media has a dedicated, fully built-out hosting page at https://cedarstreammedia.com/hosting/, titled "Managed WordPress Hosting in Sandpoint, ID," tagline "Leave the Tech to Us – Your WordPress, Managed."
- They sell their own managed WordPress hosting under a named tier, the "Sapling Package," priced at **$480/year ($40/month)**, billed as an ongoing recurring subscription (not a one-time fee).
- Advertised features: automatic WordPress core updates, plugin management (compatibility/security), regular scheduled backups, security (malware scanning, firewalls, SSL), performance optimization, LiteSpeed web server, 10GB cloud storage.
- This is a real, live, priced competing hosting product — not just a listed capability with no substance behind it. This is a materially different hosting posture than most other Sandpoint-area leads researched so far, which typically have no hosting offering at all.
- **Referral vs. white-label implication:** a pure referral pitch (as used with leads that have no hosting of their own) would directly compete with an existing revenue line here. Per PROJECT.md's white-label/agency-hosting option, this is a case where that angle — offering to operate the underlying hosting/technical infrastructure behind the scenes so Cedar Stream Media can keep billing/branding its own "Sapling"-style plan to clients — fits better than a standard referral pitch. Whether Cedar Stream Media is currently self-managing this hosting or already reselling a third-party platform under their own brand is unknown; not stated anywhere on the site.
