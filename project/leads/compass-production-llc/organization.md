## Hosting posture — strong fit finding

**Compass Production explicitly does NOT sell or bundle hosting, across every pricing tier checked.** This is close to PROJECT.md's ideal profile.

- Launch Site page (retrieved 2026-08-20): "Hosting and domain purchased separately." States plainly: "No hosting markup. No forced subscriptions. No hidden upsells. Your hosting account stays fully under your ownership and control." Recommends Hostinger (~$1.99/mo billed annually) or SiteGround as options, with no markup taken.
- Business Site page (retrieved 2026-08-20): "Client-Owned Hosting (Recommended: Hostinger)" and "Client-Owned Domain" — again no bundled hosting product.
- Growth Site page (retrieved 2026-08-20): "To keep costs low and give you full ownership, hosting and domain registration are not bundled." No recurring maintenance fee is shown on any tier beyond a stated $80/hr rate for work outside a 30-day post-launch revision window.
- No mention of a maintenance retainer, hosting resale, or ongoing subscription product anywhere in the reviewed pages (homepage, /contact, /faq, /pricing landing copy, all three tier pages).

## Why this is a strong fit

- WordPress-only shop (confirmed via generator meta tags, not just marketing copy).
- Actively recommends clients handle hosting themselves and states no markup/no forced subscription — the opposite of a competing hosting product, and exactly the "hosting isn't prominently offered" / "doesn't want the technical responsibility after launch" profile PROJECT.md describes as best fit.
- Solo operator (owner Nicholas Ries appears to be the only person involved), consistent with PROJECT.md's freelance-designer target category.
- A referral/partnership pitch would not compete with anything Compass Production currently sells — if anything, "no hosting markup, no forced subscriptions" suggests Ries may currently be fielding hosting/technical-support questions he'd rather hand off, which is the exact opening Inland Elite is looking for.
