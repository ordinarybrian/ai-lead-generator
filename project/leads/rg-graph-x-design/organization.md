## Hosting and maintenance — fit finding

**No evidence found that RG Graph X Design sells its own hosting or a website-maintenance retainer.** This is a favorable fit signal, though it comes with real caveats about how much website work this business actually does.

- No hosting, maintenance-plan, or "we manage your site" language was found anywhere on the company's own site (homepage and about page reviewed).
- Website design is listed as one service among many (logos, print, signage, apparel, video transfer) for what reads as primarily a **print and promotional-products shop** that also does branding and web work — not a dedicated web design studio. This matters for fit: PROJECT.md's best-fit profile is a designer/agency with meaningful WordPress website volume who doesn't want post-launch technical responsibility; it's unconfirmed how many WordPress client sites this business actually builds and maintains versus print/apparel work being the core revenue driver.
- The WordPress-only, no-competing-hosting-product combination is structurally the kind of fit PROJECT.md describes as ideal — the caveat is business-mix uncertainty (print/apparel shop that also does some web work), not a disqualifying platform or hosting conflict.
