## Hosting and platform — critical fit finding

- **The studio's own website is built on Squarespace** — confirmed via `images.squarespace-cdn.com` CDN URLs throughout the live site (retrieved 2026-08-20). This is first-party evidence for the studio's own site, not a client site.
- No client site's underlying platform was independently confirmed — the portfolio page displays project images/case studies but no visible platform credit, source-code inspection, or "powered by" footer language for any individual client project.
- No mention anywhere on the site of hosting, maintenance plans, retainers, or ongoing technical support being sold as a service — unlike AllerGale Design or Enleaf, there's no evidence STUDIO H already operates a competing hosting/maintenance product.
- **Working assessment:** given the studio's own site runs on Squarespace and there's no WordPress evidence anywhere on the site, the platform-fit signal leans toward a mismatch with Inland Elite's WordPress-only specialty (comparable to the Maker + Made and AllerGale Design leads, both disqualified for non-WordPress platforms) — but this is an inference from the studio's own site, not a confirmed fact about how Hara Allison builds *client* websites. A small solo print/graphic designer who occasionally builds simple websites plausibly uses Squarespace or Wix for those too (it's a common combination for a generalist), but this should be verified directly (e.g., asking, or inspecting a live client site) before treating it as settled.
