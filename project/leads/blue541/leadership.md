# Blue541 — leadership notes

- **Brad Hagadone — Owner** (per BBB business profile, fetched 2026-08-19: "Brad Hagadone, Owner"). This corroborates the original lead-index entry. See `people/brad-hagadone.md` — but note he is also the operational President/head of the entire parent Hagadone Corporation (confirmed via 2021 obituary coverage of his father, founder Duane Hagadone, in the Coeur d'Alene Press and other outlets), which makes him a high-level, low-accessibility contact rather than a typical small-agency owner-operator.
- **Mike Mortimer — General Manager** (per BBB business profile, fetched 2026-08-19: "Mike Mortimer, General Manager"; also independently findable via a LinkedIn search result for "Michael Mortimer - BLUE541"). This corroborates the original lead-index entry. See `people/mike-mortimer.md` — the more plausible day-to-day operational contact of the two.
- **Clint Schroeder — listed as "President" on the BBB profile for Blue541**, not previously in the lead-index row. Caution: independent web search found a Clint Schroeder who is President/Executive Publisher of the Hagadone Corporation's Communications Division (the newspaper/media group, ~16 papers across ID/MT/WA), with no source directly confirming a Blue541-specific title for him. This may be BBB attributing a parent-company title to the subsidiary listing, or he may genuinely hold a dual role — not resolved by this research pass. Not creating a person file for him given this ambiguity; flagging for awareness only.
- **Corporate parent — the Hagadone Corporation.** Blue541 is presented by Hagadone's own corporate site as one of its "publishing group holdings" and by Hagadone Media Group's site as "a branding and public relations division" within the Hagadone family of companies — not as an arm's-length client or independent vendor. Founder Duane B. Hagadone died in 2021; at that time Brad Hagadone (his son) was already running the parent company day-to-day. See `organization.md` for full detail and sourcing on this corporate structure, which is the key finding of this research pass.
