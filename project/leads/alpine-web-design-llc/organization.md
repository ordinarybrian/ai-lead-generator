## Hosting posture — sells its own hosting (competing product)

This is the single most important fact for qualification. Alpine Web Design LLC
**sells its own hosting and maintenance plans directly**, not just website builds:

- **Fully managed monthly plans** (their primary offering): "The Startup" at
  $100/month (1 custom page, "**Hosting & Security Included**," 1 hr/mo content
  updates) and "The Growth" at $175/month (up to 5 pages, 3 hrs/mo updates, 24/7
  priority support) — both bundle hosting into the price.
- **One-time build plans** ($700 / $1,800 / $3,000 tiers) are self-managed by default,
  but Alpine sells hosting as an add-on to these too:
  - **Managed Hosting add-on: $25/month** — "Automated backups," "Server & uptime
    monitoring."
  - Unlimited Edits add-on: $50/month.
  - Hourly support: $125/hour (1-hr minimum).

This is a direct product overlap with Inland Elite Hosting's core offering (managed
WordPress hosting, maintenance, migrations, support) — not the "hosting gap" the
freelancer/designer target category is meant to find. Alpine's $25/month standalone
hosting add-on is also priced below Inland Elite's $29/month Standard tier.

## Platform mismatch

Alpine hand-codes every site (HTML/CSS/JS) rather than building on WordPress. Inland
Elite's core service (managed **WordPress** hosting/maintenance/migrations) has no
direct product to offer a hand-coded static/custom site in the first place — a second,
independent mismatch on top of the hosting overlap.

## Why this is not a good fit (comparable precedent)

This matches the pattern of other already-disqualified leads in this pipeline with the
same structure — a solo hand-coded-site developer who already bundles hosting at or
below Inland Elite's pricing:

- **Pinecode Creations** (Spokane, solo, hand-coded, $25/month bundled hosting) —
  disqualified 2026-08-11 by Brian: "already bundles his own hosting... which is
  cheaper than Inland Elite's hosting tiers."
- **2Dudes Digital Design** and **A Nerd's Place** — both disqualified for "already
  sells its own hosting... a direct product overlap."

Alpine Web Design LLC fits this same profile essentially point-for-point: solo
operator, hand-coded (non-WordPress) sites, and an in-house hosting product priced at
or below Inland Elite's own tiers.
