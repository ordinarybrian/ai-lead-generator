## Services and positioning

- Core services (from site, `/services/` and `/about-us/`): website design/development, graphic design, logo design, print design, social media management, email marketing, SEO (organic and paid), and media buying/production.
- Positioning: "cutting-edge and affordable web designs," long local tenure ("14 years and over 200 local clients" appears alongside the "16 years" framing on the homepage — the two figures aren't reconciled, treat as marketing copy, not a precise timeline), most staff "have been with us since our company inception" (retention as a selling point).
- Service-area copy is broad and reads as SEO keyword-targeting rather than a literal claim of physical operations — page titles and body text list Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls, Sandpoint, and Spokane consistently, but some pages/titles also list Seattle, Portland, Boise, and Missoula. Treat the CDA/Post Falls/Spokane footprint as the real one and the wider list as SEO reach, not confirmed physical service capacity.

## Hosting service — key open question, now confirmed

- The site explicitly and specifically sells hosting as a named service line (not just "we can host it if you need"). Per `/services/` (fetched 2026-08-19), the hosting package includes:
  - 1 hour of free website updates per month
  - Up to 200 professional email accounts
  - A website statistics package "available 24/7"
  - "No contracts"
  - Stated compatibility with "most platforms"
  - In-house/local technical support (site explicitly contrasts this with offshore call centers)
  - "Fast, secure, and reliable servers" (marketing language, no technical specifics)
- **No pricing or named plan tiers are published anywhere found** (site, search results). A targeted search for hosting pricing returned nothing Knock-specific.
- This confirms and sharpens the existing lead-index note: Knock does not just dabble in hosting, it markets a bundled hosting+email+support product to its own clients. A plain referral pitch ("send us your hosting clients") would compete directly with a real, actively-marketed revenue line — this is a white-label/backend-infrastructure conversation only, not a hosting hand-off, exactly as the existing row's notes anticipated.

## Notable / caution: "KMD Medical Web Design" sub-brand

- Independently confirmed via search: a business called **"KMD Medical Design"** (kmdmedicaldesign.com, also on Facebook as "KMD Medical Design") exists and markets custom (non-template) medical-practice website design across "Seattle, Spokane and Coeur d'Alene, Idaho" — the same three-city footprint pattern Knock uses.
- This is consistent with (but does not itself prove) the existing lead-index note that KMD shares Knock's exact address/phone one suite over (#258 vs #259) and is almost certainly a Knock sub-brand targeting medical clients specifically, not a separate company. Not independently verified beyond the existing Google Maps note; not logged as its own lead row, per the existing guidance.
