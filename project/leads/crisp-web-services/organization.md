## Hosting posture — partial overlap, worth noting but not disqualifying

**Crisp Web Services does not operate its own branded hosting product, but it does offer to manage hosting for clients through a third party (SiteGround) if the client wants that handled for them.** This is a softer, more common form of overlap than a proprietary CMS/hosting product (compare AllerGale Design's Pixelcactus/Lotzabuzz, which is a hard disqualifier).

- FAQ page (crispwebservices.com/crisp-website-design/faq/, retrieved 2026-08-20): "We personally use and highly recommend Siteground... You can also choose to go through us to get a hosting package. We will purchase and manage everything for you."
- No pricing, markup, or recurring monthly hosting fee is disclosed anywhere in the reviewed content — this reads as an optional convenience for clients who don't want to set up hosting themselves, not a dedicated monetized hosting/maintenance product with its own brand or pricing page (unlike Social Strategix's $99/month "Worry-Free Cloud Hosting" or AllerGale's Pixelcactus).
- The site does separately list "Website Edits & Maintenance" as a service category, described generically (content updates, form setup, security fixes, one-time or monthly plans) without hosting specifics attached.

## Why this matters for fit

- WordPress-only shop with a genuine solo-freelancer profile (Jessica Crisp is the only person named, "when you call it's me that answers") — structurally a good match for PROJECT.md's freelance-designer target category.
- The SiteGround pass-through offer is the one complicating factor: she already has an answer for clients who ask about hosting, even though it's informal (no branded product, no stated markup, third-party host). This is closer to the "hasn't formalized hosting as a business line but already fields the question" pattern than to a hard channel conflict. A referral pitch should probably acknowledge this rather than assume she has no hosting story at all — but it's not disqualifying, since there's no competing branded/monetized product to protect.
- No evidence of a maintenance retainer with disclosed pricing; the "Website Edits & Maintenance" and "Stride Marketing" services are more general marketing/edit work than infrastructure/hosting specifically.
