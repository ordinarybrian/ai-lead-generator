## Services and positioning

- Per the company's own homepage: a broad multimedia/creative shop — photography (portrait, product, modeling, wedding, lifestyle, drone), video production (commercials, event coverage, motion graphics, music videos, training content), design (branding, **website UI/UX**, packaging, layout, illustration), marketing (SEO, Google Ads, social media campaigns, copywriting), and even some business-consulting language (dental office operations, startup strategy).
- Website-related services exist ("Website Development" / "website UI/UX design" / "ecommerce site deployment" appear in site copy and portfolio nav), but are one line item among many — this reads as a multimedia/branding studio that *also* builds websites, not a dedicated web design shop. This is closer to PROJECT.md's "graphic/brand designers who occasionally build websites" category than the "freelance web designer" best-fit category.
- Founder: Halvor Halversen, described in search results as "CEO // Creative Director of OctoFox" with a BFA in Graphic Design (Pacific Union College, 2012–2016).

## Hosting and platform — fit finding

- The company's **own website is built on Webflow** — confirmed via `cdn.prod.website-files.com` CDN URLs (retrieved 2026-08-20). This is first-party evidence for OctoFox's own site only.
- No client website's underlying platform (WordPress vs. Webflow vs. other) was independently confirmed — the portfolio/design pages reviewed did not credit a CMS per project.
- **No mention anywhere on the site of hosting, maintenance retainers, or ongoing technical/website support being sold as a product.** This is a positive signal versus AllerGale Design or Front ROI (see that lead's file) — no evidence of a competing hosting/maintenance offering to navigate around.
- Working assessment: platform fit is unconfirmed rather than disqualifying. If OctoFox builds client sites on Webflow (as their own site is), that would be a structural mismatch with Inland Elite's WordPress specialty; if they build client WordPress sites and just chose Webflow for their own marketing site, there's no mismatch. This needs direct confirmation (e.g., in a first conversation) before being treated as settled either way.
