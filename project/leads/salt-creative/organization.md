- The existing lead-index.csv note describes this as a "Sandpoint creative and SEO studio." That appears to be a mischaracterization based on one of the site's geo-targeted local-SEO landing pages, `/web-design-sandpoint-idaho`, which markets to the Sandpoint market ("we've worked with business owners... in the region for nearly 7 years") but does not claim a physical Sandpoint office or address.
- The company runs the same kind of landing page for many other markets: Boise, Idaho (state-wide), California, Arizona, Oregon, Montana, Washington, Utah, Colorado, plus industry-specific pages (real estate, construction). This is a multi-state programmatic-SEO content strategy from a single Boise HQ, not evidence of a local North Idaho presence.
- No physical address, office, or local phone number tied to Sandpoint or anywhere in North Idaho was found anywhere on the site or in search results. Treat "Sandpoint" as a service-area marketing target only, not a location fact.
