- Broad "full-service" digital marketing services list: SEO (local + national), PPC, social media marketing, content marketing, email marketing, video marketing, logo design, Google Maps/Google Business Profile optimization, reputation management, and web design (custom, responsive, e-commerce-capable).
- Web design services page states they offer "ongoing website maintenance to keep your site running smoothly" as part of web design services, but gives no detail on what that includes, what platform sites are built on (no mention of WordPress specifically or any other CMS), maintenance-plan pricing, or support tiers.

- Blog consists entirely of programmatic, near-identical "Digital Marketing In [Small Washington Town]" posts (Spokane Valley, Waverly, Clayton, Four Lakes, Loon Lake, Nine Mile Falls, Deer Park, Medical Lake, Liberty Lake, Airway Heights), all dated within a few days of each other in January 2025 — a pattern typical of mass-produced local-SEO landing-page content rather than organic posts from real staff. No author bylines on any post.

## Credibility / network signals (caution)

- The site's footer (checked via the `/about/` page) cross-links to a large, eclectic set of unrelated local-service business websites with no topical connection to marketing or Post Falls — e.g., forklift sales/repair, RV repair/paint, pool maintenance, a smoke shop, tree trimming/landscaping, van conversion kits, solar panel cleaning, and windshield repair — alongside links to `curtismelancon.com` (a digital marketing consultant based in Orange County, CA), `developingprospects.com` (a prior company of that same consultant), and `meticulousjessmarketing.com` (a similarly-patterned "[name] marketing" site targeting Tampa, FL).
- This is a classic reciprocal-footer-link pattern associated with a single web developer/SEO vendor building and cross-linking many unrelated small-business sites for backlink SEO value — it suggests postfallsmarketing.com may have been built and is possibly operated/maintained by an outside SEO vendor (plausibly Orange-County-CA-connected, given the Curtis Melancon link and the 949 phone number noted above) rather than being a hands-on, independently-run North Idaho shop.
- No LinkedIn company page found for "Post Falls Marketing" despite a direct search.
- Not listed in the BBB's Post Falls, ID marketing-consultant category (checked directly).
- No customer reviews located via search under this business name/phone number.
- None of this proves the business isn't real or isn't locally run — plenty of small agencies have thin web/social presence — but the combination (no named staff anywhere, residential-looking address, out-of-state-linked footer network, programmatic blog content, no LinkedIn, no BBB listing) is a materially different risk profile than a typical small local shop and should be weighed accordingly.
