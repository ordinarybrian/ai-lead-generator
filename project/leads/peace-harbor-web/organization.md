## Services and structure

Peace Harbor operates as a family of subdomains under peaceharbor.com, each apparently a distinct service line of the same company:

- `web.peaceharbor.com` — Peace Harbor Web: website design, built primarily on WordPress with the Divi theme; also branding/logo creation, content strategy, social media integration, WordPress SEO (RankMath, Semrush mentioned).
- `hosting.peaceharbor.com` — Peace Harbor Web Hosting: **their own hosting product**, with published tiered pricing (see "Hosting posture" below).
- `designs.peaceharbor.com` — Peace Harbor Designs: graphic design (logos, branding, flyers, brochures, business cards, web graphics, podcast artwork, menus, CD/DVD design, etc.).
- `printing.peaceharbor.com` — Peace Harbor Printing: digital and offset printing (banners, signage, menus, business cards, postcards, flyers, brochures, doorhangers).
- `publishing.peaceharbor.com` — Peace Harbor Publishing: author self-publishing services (cover design, proofreading, editing, formatting for print/Kindle, ISBN setup, eBook conversion).
- `clients.peaceharbor.com` — appears to be a client billing/shopping-cart portal (e.g. `/store/web-hosting/`).

Broader service list per search-summary of the company "About" page: web design, web hosting, graphic design, digital printing, IT support, and online/social marketing — serving "businesses, ministries, and creatives" and, per one snippet, "individual users and businesses across the United States and Canada," not purely a local Sandpoint client base.

Also sells WordPress maintenance/update subscriptions starting at $50/month (their own competing maintenance-plan product, separate from hosting).

## Hosting posture — DISQUALIFYING

Peace Harbor sells its own web hosting directly and prominently, under its own branded subdomain (`hosting.peaceharbor.com`), with published tiered pricing (per search-summary of that subdomain's pages, not independently fetched):

- **Starter** — $13/mo or $125/yr — 1GB storage, unlimited bandwidth/email/FTP, free SSL, nightly backups, cPanel, "expert Cloudflare management."
- **Builder** — $21/mo or $200/yr.
- **Grow** — $29/mo or $275/yr — 5GB storage, unlimited everything else.
- 20% discount for paying annually; additional 20% off for churches/faith-based ministries/nonprofits.

This is not an incidental or legacy offering — it's a full, actively marketed hosting product line with its own subdomain, tiered pricing, and a dedicated "About" and "Contact" page, plus a $50+/month maintenance-plan product on top of it. Given the project's stated disqualifying condition (an agency that already sells its own hosting/maintenance is a direct competitor, not a referral fit — see PROJECT.md target categories), **Peace Harbor Web is a direct competitor to Inland Elite Hosting's core offering, not a referral partner.**

The name "Peace Harbor Web" itself is exactly what PROJECT.md's research brief flagged as worth checking — and the check confirms the concern: they are, in effect, a competing managed-hosting provider that also happens to do web design, not a designer who avoids hosting responsibility.

Note: PROJECT.md does describe a white-label/reseller option Inland Elite can offer to agencies that already sell their own hosting/maintenance, specifically to avoid this exact competitive conflict. That is a theoretical path back into consideration for this lead if ever revisited, but it was not evaluated further here since the task was fit/disqualification research, not outreach strategy.
