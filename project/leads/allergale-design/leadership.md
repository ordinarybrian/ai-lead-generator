## Ownership discrepancy — resolution (partial)

The existing CSV `fit_reason` flagged that "BBB lists Aller, ZoomInfo lists Gale as co-owner" as unconfirmed/conflicting. This pass found more detail but not a full resolution:

- **BBB** (https://www.bbb.org/us/id/sandpoint/profile/graphic-designer/allergale-design-1296-1000178552, retrieved 2026-08-10) lists the business type as **Sole Proprietorship** and names **Mr. Shawn Aller** as the sole "Owner" / principal contact. A sole proprietorship can only have one legal owner of record, so this is likely a description of the legal filing structure, not necessarily a claim that Sandy Gale has no real ownership stake or authority in the business.
- The company's **own site** (allergale.com/about, retrieved 2026-08-10) presents both as parallel, equally titled co-owners: Shawn Aller — "Co-Owner, Web & Graphic Designer, Website Developer"; Sandy Gale — "Co-Owner, Web & Graphic Designer, Illustrator, Technical Writer." AllerGale.online (a second AllerGale-operated marketing landing page) repeats the same co-owner framing for both.
- **ZoomInfo** (search-result snippets only; direct page fetch returned HTTP 403) similarly describes Sandy Gale as "Co-Owner and Designer at AllerGale Design."
- **LinkedIn's company page** (linkedin.com/company/allergale-design, fetched 2026-08-10, partial) lists only Shawn Aller as a named/associated employee — Sandy Gale does not appear on the company page itself, though her personal LinkedIn profile (found via search only, direct fetch returned HTTP 999/blocked) is titled "Co-Owner / Designer, AllerGale Design."
- Shawn Aller is also the one associated with the studio's **technical/platform side**: he operates under the Webflow handle **@pixelcactus** (member since 2013, bio: "We create amazing Websites and Graphic Designs for businesses all around the USA," location Sandpoint, ID — https://webflow.com/@pixelcactus, fetched 2026-08-10), and both of AllerGale's self-branded hosting/CMS platforms (Pixelcactus and Lotzabuzz — see organization.md) appear to be his initiative under the AllerGale umbrella. Client-site credits (e.g. accafrica.org/site-credits) name both Shawn Aller and Sandy Gale jointly as designers/developers, so this isn't a claim that Sandy is uninvolved in web work — just that Shawn is the more consistently technical/platform-facing half.

**Working conclusion:** genuinely a two-person, co-run studio in practice (both are credited jointly on client sites, both hold parallel "Co-Owner" titles on the company's own materials), but Shawn Aller is the one named as legal owner of record (BBB), the one tied to the company's LinkedIn presence, and the one associated with the underlying technical platforms (Pixelcactus, Lotzabuzz) that are most relevant to a hosting conversation. This is not a full resolution of "who is more senior" — treat both as genuine co-owners for relationship purposes, but Shawn Aller is the better-supported choice for a first, hosting/technical-focused contact.
