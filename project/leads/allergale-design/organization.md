## Hosting and maintenance — critical fit finding

**AllerGale Design operates two of its own branded, monetized website-platform/hosting products, not just an occasional maintenance add-on. This is a significant, disqualifying overlap with Inland Elite's core offering.**

- **Pixelcactus** (https://www.pixelcactus.com/), retrieved 2026-08-10 — its own footer states "pixelcactus is a service provided by AllerGale Design," and its contact page lists the identical address (502 W Cedar, Suite G, Sandpoint, ID) and phone number as AllerGale Design's BBB listing — same entity, not a separate company. Founded 2012 per a third-party company-info aggregator (The Company Check; not independently confirmed beyond the aggregator). Pixelcactus is a proprietary CMS/website platform, not WordPress, that bundles hosting: "Hosting via Amazon Web Services and CloudFront with SSL security," plus Zoho email/webmail integration, content management, SEO tools, and email marketing.
  - Pricing page (https://www.pixelcactus.com/pricing, retrieved 2026-08-10): **$49/month** (or $45/month billed annually, one free month) for "Monthly CMS website hosting," described as including a fast CDN, SSL certificate ("a value of over $100/year"), 2 email accounts + business workplace app suite, 3 contributor logins, and automatic backup/restore. This is a directly monetized hosting product sold to AllerGale's own web design clients.
- **Lotzabuzz** (https://lotzabuzz.com/), retrieved 2026-08-10 — footer copyright reads "© 2022-2026 lotzabuzz by AllerGale Design," confirming common ownership. Described (via search-result summary) as a newer, more full-featured website builder/marketing platform: page/funnel builder, e-commerce, CRM, membership management, email automation. At least one real client site (African Conservation Centre, accafrica.org) is independently confirmed as "designed and developed by Shawn Aller & Sandy Gale of AllerGale Design," "built on the Lotzabuzz platform" (per that site's own /site-credits page, retrieved 2026-08-10) — direct, first-party-adjacent confirmation that Lotzabuzz is a live, in-use product, not just marketing copy.
- A third domain, **AllerGale.online** (https://www.allergale.online/, retrieved 2026-08-10), is a "Website Builder & Marketing Platform" landing page that also identifies itself as operated by AllerGale Design and names both Shawn Aller and Sandy Gale — this reads as an alternate marketing/SEO landing page for the same core business rather than a clearly distinct third hosting product, but it reinforces that hosting/platform sales are central to how they market themselves, not an incidental add-on.
- The company's own /about page states they offer "maintenance plans for every budget" as part of "technical support services" for clients who don't want to manage their own site — consistent with, and likely referring to, the Pixelcactus/Lotzabuzz platform subscriptions above rather than a separate hands-off maintenance-only product.
- No mention of WordPress anywhere in reviewed site content — the platform is a proprietary CMS, not WordPress, which is a structural mismatch with Inland Elite's managed-**WordPress**-hosting specialty (comparable to the Maker + Made lead, disqualified for a Squarespace/Shopify platform mismatch).

## Why this is not a plausible fit

- PROJECT.md's ideal freelance-designer/studio target is one where "hosting isn't prominently offered" and infrastructure is a burden they'd rather hand off. AllerGale Design is the opposite: hosting and a proprietary CMS platform (via Pixelcactus and Lotzabuzz) appear to be a deliberate, actively marketed, monetized part of their business model, not a side effect of doing web design.
- This is a stronger disqualifier than the "already sells a maintenance retainer" pattern seen with Adapt Digital Solutions or Ever Marketing (where a white-label backend angle was still considered worth raising): those competitors' underlying sites were still WordPress, leaving room for a backend-infrastructure conversation. AllerGale's platforms are proprietary, non-WordPress CMSs — Inland Elite's specialty doesn't map cleanly onto that stack at all.
- A referral/partnership pitch (the primary ask per PROJECT.md) would ask them to send clients to a hosting provider that competes directly with a product they already sell under two of their own brands. Even the standing white-label option (PROJECT.md's "Optional Commercial Terms" section) is a weak fit here, since they're not reselling someone else's hosting — they've built and host their own platform stack.
