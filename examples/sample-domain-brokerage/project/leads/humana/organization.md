# Humana

## Organization

- Organization: Humana Inc.
- Website: https://www.humana.com/
- Organization type: Publicly traded (NYSE: HUM) health insurance company. Major Medicare Advantage insurer in the U.S., with CenterWell as its healthcare-delivery subsidiary (senior-focused primary care, pharmacy, home health).
- CEO: Jim (James) Rechtin — became President & CEO effective July 2024 (joined the company January 2024 as President & COO). Not the recommended contact per role-priority rules; noted for context only.
- Relevant audience/market: Medicare-eligible seniors (Humana's core membership base), their caregivers/family members, and the broader Medicare Advantage market. Humana explicitly markets senior-focused wellness, social, and community programming, not just insurance.

## Note: do not confuse with Humana AB (Sweden)

Search results for "Humana + Chief Marketing Officer" also surface **Humana AB**, a Swedish care-services company (appointed Gabriella Reuterswärd as CMO) — this is an unrelated company. All research below is specific to **Humana Inc.**, the U.S. health insurer at humana.com, which is the correct target given the lead-index entry (organization type: Healthcare/Medicare).

## CORRECTION (2026-08-06, post-draft): Humana Neighborhood Centers is discontinued

The "strong strategic fit" section below originally treated Humana Neighborhood Centers as the strongest live angle. **That is now confirmed wrong.** Humana Neighborhood Center virtual and in-person programming was discontinued as of **September 18, 2025**, and humananeighborhoodcenter.com is no longer active. Verified via web search 2026-08-06 across multiple independent sources: [thelayoff.com](https://www.thelayoff.com/t/1rgmyrK5) ("All Humana Neighborhood Centers to close" — Humana Inc. layoffs discussion) and [community.medicare.go365.com](https://community.medicare.go365.com/s/article/Humana-Neighborhood-Centers) (Humana's own member-community article on the closure). The original research below (organization.md as first drafted, plus leadership.md and people/jennifer-ilecki.md) leaned on this program as the central outreach hook — **do not use Humana Neighborhood Centers in any outreach copy.**

**CenterWell Senior Primary Care activity centers remain active and current** (re-verified 2026-08-06): open to the public, not just patients, hosting bingo, movie nights, fitness classes, and health education, with a zip-code-searchable events page at https://www.centerwellprimarycare.com/en/events (note: the bare domain centerwellprimarycare.com without "www." does not resolve — use the full URL). This is now the strongest current angle.

**Also newly found (2026-08-06), not yet used in outreach:** Humana Community Navigator, at humana.findhelp.com, powered by findhelp.org — a broad social-needs directory (600,000+ programs: food, housing, transportation, legal, employment), searchable by zip code, open to members and non-members. This is not senior-center-specific and is a weaker literal match than CenterWell, but is a real, current, zip-searchable Humana tool worth knowing about as a fallback angle or second-touch material.

- **Points of Caregiving** — a Humana caregiver-support program aimed at the nation's family caregivers, alongside an existing "Caring for Others" resource hub at humana.com/caring-for-others. Not re-verified 2026-08-06.
- **SilverSneakers** — Humana also offers/promotes SilverSneakers fitness and social programming for members. Not re-verified 2026-08-06.

In short: Humana already operates the real-world thing SeniorCenter.com's positioning describes (a network of local senior centers with directory search, activities, and caregiver resources) — it is not a stretch to imagine SeniorCenter.com as a consumer-facing umbrella brand or directory front door for these programs, distinct from the insurance-sales-heavy humana.com main site. As of 2026-08-06, CenterWell is the program actually carrying this argument; Neighborhood Centers no longer can.

This is a working strategic hypothesis, not a claim about Humana's current plans or budget priorities.
