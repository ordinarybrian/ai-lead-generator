## Potential SeniorCenter.com use (working hypothesis)

APFM is a referral marketplace: its economics depend on reaching families *early* in the care-search journey. SeniorCenter.com could work as a consumer-facing front door — a place families land when they're searching for local senior resources (centers, programs, care options) before they know the A Place for Mom brand. Less "mission destination" (AARP framing), more top-of-funnel acquisition and brand property.

This is a working hypothesis, not a claim about APFM's plans.
