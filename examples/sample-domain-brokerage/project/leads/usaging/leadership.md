## Seed contact assessment: Sandy Markwood — confirmed as the right primary contact

The lead-index seed data named Sandy Markwood, CEO, as the primary contact, with the note that her title was unverified. This is now confirmed:

- Sandy Markwood is publicly and consistently identified as USAging's Chief Executive Officer across the org's own staff page, her official bio page (usaging.org), her LinkedIn profile, and numerous USAging LinkedIn posts referring to her as "USAging CEO Sandy Markwood."
- She has held the role since 2002 — roughly 24 years of tenure, indicating stability and institutional authority.

USAging has no CMO, Chief Growth Officer, Chief Digital Officer, or Chief Commercial Officer — the entire senior staff list above is it, and none of the other four Chiefs (Finance, Communications, Programs/Services, Policy/Membership) rank above CEO in the project's role-priority order for an organization this size. Per the project's own explicit caveat — "CEO, when the organization is small or when other executive outreach fails" — USAging is exactly this case: a small nonprofit association (five-person C-suite; the full staff directory captured here runs well under 20 named people) with no marketing-specific executive at all.

**Recommendation: keep Sandy Markwood as the primary contact.** This is a confirmation, not a swap.
