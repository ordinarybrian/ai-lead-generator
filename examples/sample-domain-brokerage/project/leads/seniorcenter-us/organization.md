# SeniorCenter.us

## Organization

- Organization: SeniorCenter.us (no separate legal/company name found)
- Website: https://seniorcenter.us/
- Organization type: Ad-supported national senior care directory (self-described as "an independent informational directory")
- Discovered: 2026-08-06, incidentally, while fact-checking the "no single place online" claim used in outreach to NCOA and other prospects.

## What it is (verified via direct fetch 2026-08-06)

- Homepage: "Find Senior Centers and Senior Programs Near You" — searchable by state, city, zip code, or center name.
- Scale: 8,271 senior center listings; 44,496 total listings across five care categories; all 50 states + D.C. represented (ranging from 11 listings in D.C. to 576 in California).
- About page states the mission as "Helping families find senior care and community services," explicitly disclaims operating any listed organization or providing medical/legal/financial/referral services — positions itself as a neutral directory, not a service provider.
- Ad-supported: has an advertising disclosure section; also accepts provider-submitted listing claims/corrections, suggesting some provider-side monetization too.
- Professionally built: clean navigation, resource guides, listing-verification language, provider tools (claim listing, add listing, provider login). Not a bare SEO placeholder.
- Coverage is not complete relative to the ~11,000 senior centers estimated to exist nationally (per NCOA-sourced figures — see leads/national-council-on-aging/organization.md), so it is a real but partial directory, not an exhaustive one.

## Ownership — unresolved

No founder, company name, or parent organization disclosed on the homepage, About page, or Contact page (checked directly 2026-08-06). No LinkedIn company page found via search. This looks like a genuinely anonymous, ad-supported directory operation — possibly a single-site operator or part of an undisclosed portfolio of similar niche directory sites (a common structure in the ad-supported local-directory business). Not yet confirmed either way.

## Why this is relevant to SeniorCenter.com as a prospect (working hypothesis)

Whoever operates SeniorCenter.us is the one party already running the literal business SeniorCenter.com's positioning describes elsewhere in this pipeline. A plausible buyer angle: upgrading from .us to .com is a direct brand/SEO/type-in-traffic play, and would eliminate the naming confusion between the two properties. This is speculative — without a named operator, there's no way to assess budget, ambition, or actual interest in an acquisition.

## Why this also matters for every other lead in the pipeline

The core pitch used across AARP, A Place for Mom, Brookdale, SilverAssist, and the Humana/NCOA drafts leans on some version of "there's no single place online to find a senior center." That claim is not literally true — SeniorCenter.us already exists and does a partial version of this. It doesn't kill the pitch (SeniorCenter.us has no name recognition, no backing from a known brand, and incomplete coverage), but any outreach claiming *no such site exists anywhere* is now factually checkable and wrong. Language should shift to something narrower and still true: "nothing carries [org]'s own name/backing" or "nothing that's actually part of [org]'s own network," rather than "there's no single place online."
