# SilverAssist

## Organization

- Organization: SilverAssist (private, backed by Growth Catalyst Partners)
- Website: https://www.silverassist.com
- Organization type: Senior-assistance platform combining advisory, placement, and financial services.
- Formed 2021 when Growth Catalyst Partners combined ElderLife Financial Services, Care Changes, and FamilyAssets.
- Scale (per formation-era reporting, likely larger now): ~10,000 families reached per month, contractual relationships with 7,000+ communities, 130,000+ older adults helped.

## Portfolio / acquisition history

- **2021** — formed from ElderLife Financial Services, Care Changes, FamilyAssets.
- **October 2023** — acquired Oasis Senior Advisors (national network of local Certified Senior Advisors; founded 2014 by Tim Evankovich).
- **January 12, 2026** — acquired Caring.com, positioned as the platform's digital-demand-generation layer. See `leads/caring-com/organization.md` for full detail on that deal and the outreach-authority uncertainty it raised.

SilverAssist is an active, serial acquirer of eldercare-adjacent businesses — three acquisitions/combinations in five years, each expanding either digital reach, local advisory presence, or financial services. A domain acquisition is not a strange ask for this buyer; it fits the pattern of what they already do.

## Why the pivot from Cindy Starr/Caring.com

Caring.com's own announcement quoted only SilverAssist and Oasis Senior Advisors executives (Greg Mason, John Benbrook) on strategy — not anyone from Caring.com itself. That, plus Caring.com being explicitly folded into a larger combined strategy rather than run standalone, suggests real decisions about a digital asset like SeniorCenter.com are more likely to sit with SilverAssist leadership than with Caring.com's Chief Growth Officer post-acquisition. Going to the parent directly is more likely to reach an actual decision-maker.

## Potential SeniorCenter.com use (working hypothesis)

SilverAssist's stated strategy is combining digital scale (Caring.com) with local advisory (Oasis) and financing (ElderLife). SeniorCenter.com could work as an additional national digital front door feeding into that same combined pipeline — one more top-of-funnel entry point ahead of the advisory/placement/financing services they already offer downstream.

This is a working hypothesis, not a claim about SilverAssist's plans.
