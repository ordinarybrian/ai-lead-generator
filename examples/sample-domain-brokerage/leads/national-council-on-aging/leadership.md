## Reconciling the seed contact: Constance ("Connie") Weaver

The lead-index seed data listed Constance Weaver as NCOA's primary_contact with title unknown. Research shows this is **not a staff role**:

- Weaver is an NCOA **volunteer board member** — currently Treasurer, and per NCOA's own announcement (ncoa.org/article/ncoa-congratulates-connie-weaver-as-new-chair-elect/), elected to become Board **Chair-Elect effective July 1, 2026** — a date that has already passed as of this research (2026-08-06), so she should currently hold the Chair-Elect role.
- Her actual day job is **Chief Marketing Officer of Equitable** (a financial services/insurance company), not NCOA. Her career includes senior marketing roles at TIAA (Senior EVP & Chief Marketing & Communications Officer), The Hartford (SVP & CMO), and AT&T (EVP & CMO), plus earlier roles at Microsoft, McGraw-Hill, BearingPoint, and MCI. She also sits on the boards of Make-A-Wish America, the National Endowment for Financial Education, and Connecticut Public Media.

**Assessment:** Weaver has a genuinely elite marketing/CMO background — exactly the kind of profile this project's role-priority order is built around — but at a different organization. She has no operational authority over NCOA's website, digital properties, or budget; she is a governance/oversight volunteer, not an NCOA employee. Following this project's rule that the ideal contact "owns brand, growth, digital strategy, customer acquisition, or marketing investment" at the target organization itself, **she is not recommended as the primary outreach target**, despite being the seed contact. This is flagged explicitly rather than silently swapped, per instructions.

She could plausibly be useful later as a secondary/warm-referral channel (e.g., a LinkedIn note after staff outreach has been tried) given her marketing background and board seniority — but that would be a deliberate secondary tactic, not the default first move.

## Who fits the role-priority order instead

NCOA's executive team currently has **no title matching** CMO, Chief Growth Officer, Chief Digital Officer, Chief Commercial Officer, or EVP/SVP of Marketing. The closest available fits, in order:

1. **Todd Pittman — Chief Development Officer.** His stated mandate ("strategic partnerships, build revenue") maps most directly onto role-priority tier #7 (Strategic Partnerships leadership). A secondary source surfaced via search (not independently confirmed by direct fetch this session — see `sources.md`) suggested his "Advancement Division" also encompasses Marketing & Communications and Digital teams; if accurate, he would functionally be NCOA's top marketing/digital executive despite the Development title. This claim should be re-verified before leaning on it heavily.
2. **Ramsey Alwin — President & CEO.** Role-priority tier #8, normally a last resort. Justified here as the natural alternate specifically because NCOA has no dedicated marketing/growth/digital executive at all — the "when other routes fail" condition is effectively already true for this organization, unlike AARP, A Place for Mom, or Brookdale, where a clean CMO exists.
3. **Daniel Reiner — Associate Director of Marketing, Corporate Sponsorship & Membership** (found via a LinkedIn search-result title only, not independently researched further). A genuinely marketing-titled staffer, but too junior to be a credible first-touch contact for an asset-level conversation. Keep as a distant fallback only.
4. **Josh Hodges — Chief Impact Officer.** Considered and set aside as primary: his mandate (programmatic work, research, government affairs, campaigns) is audience/reach-oriented in spirit but not a marketing, growth, digital, or partnerships title, so it doesn't map as cleanly to the role-priority order as Pittman's does.
