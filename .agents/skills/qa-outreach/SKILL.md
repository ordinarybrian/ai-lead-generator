---
name: qa-outreach
description: Audit a finished outreach draft against the project's fact, consent, and writing rules. Use when reviewing or approving an outreach draft, not when researching or drafting it.
---

Read the draft, `project/email-rules/email-qa.md`, and the minimum lead context required for Test 0. Read `project/email-rules/email-writing-rules.md` when the QA checklist directs you to its rules.

- Start with fact and consent checks. A `do_not_contact=TRUE`, stale fact, or untraceable personalization detail is an immediate failure.
- Evaluate every required QA test. Do not mark a draft pass based only on tone or word count.
- Report the exact failed test and the sentence or fact that caused it, then state the required fix.
- A QA pass approves only the draft's readiness for user review. It does not authorize sending outreach.
