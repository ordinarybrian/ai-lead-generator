---
name: lead-research
description: Research and grade a prospective organization or decision-maker before outreach. Use for lead discovery, organization research, or person research; not for drafting or QA.
---

Preserve the distinction between verified facts, sourced inferences, and pattern-based guesses.

- Read `project/PROJECT.md` before evaluating a project-specific prospect.
- For discovery or grading, read `project/leads/README.md`, inspect the CSV schema, and search for duplicate or near-duplicate organizations before adding a row.
- For organization or person research, use only that organization's research folder and the sources needed to verify the requested facts.
- Verify a contact's current title and employment before outreach work when `last_verified` is stale.
- Do not invent contact details, business facts, strategic priorities, or sources. Label email confidence separately from delivery status.
- Do not draft outreach or contact anyone. Hand off to `draft-outreach` only when the user asks for a draft.
