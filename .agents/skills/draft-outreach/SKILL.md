---
name: draft-outreach
description: Draft a personalized outreach email for a researched lead. Use only when the user asks to create or revise outreach copy.
---

Before drafting, read the relevant organization and person research, the lead's CSV row, `project/PROJECT.md`, and `project/email-rules/email-writing-rules.md`.

- Stop and flag the record if `do_not_contact` is `TRUE`.
- Re-verify stale title, employment, and personalization facts before relying on them.
- Keep sourced facts traceable; do not fill gaps with assumptions.
- Follow the project's contact priority, commercial confidentiality, and first-contact boundaries.
- Save drafts under `project/outreach/<organization-slug>/<person-slug>/` when creating files.
- Drafting is never authorization to send, publish, attach assets, accept an offer, or update a sent-status.
