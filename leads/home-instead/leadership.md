## Role-priority assessment

Per the project's contact-selection order (CMO > Chief Growth Officer > Chief Digital Officer > Chief Commercial Officer > EVP/SVP Marketing > VP Marketing/Digital/Brand/Growth > Strategic Partnerships > CEO-if-small-org):

- **No Chief Marketing Officer, Chief Growth Officer, Chief Digital Officer, or Chief Commercial Officer exists** on Honor/Home Instead's current published leadership team.
- **Kim Atkinson (Chief Communications Officer)** is the closest fit available. Multiple independent sources (her SilverEconomyForum speaker bio, LinkedIn-derived summaries, and a 2026 Newsweek-recognition quote attributed to her) describe her as leading "brand, marketing, and communications" for the combined company, and specifically credit her with leading the 2023-era Home Instead brand relaunch — i.e., she functions as the top brand/marketing decision-maker even though her formal title is communications-focused, not "CMO."
- Stefan Haney's "SVP of Growth Technology" title (from a single news article, not the current leadership page) sounds adjacent to "growth" but is very likely a product/engineering-oriented growth-tech role (typical for a home-care matching platform), not a marketing/brand/acquisition-strategy role — not recommended as primary contact without more evidence of scope.
- No VP of Marketing, Digital, or Brand distinct from Atkinson was found.

## Recommendation

**Kim Atkinson (Kim Atkinson Van Pelt on LinkedIn) — Chief Communications Officer, Honor Technology and Home Instead** is the recommended primary contact. This is the seed contact from lead-index.csv, and research confirms she is (a) still active in the role as of 2026, and (b) functionally the senior-most brand/marketing person at the company, even though CCO sits below the project's ideal "CMO" tier. Given no better-fitting title exists at this organization, and given the person is explicitly credited with brand strategy work, she satisfies the intent of the role-priority rule ("owns brand, growth, digital strategy, customer acquisition, or marketing investment") even if her literal title does not appear on the priority list.

No internal backup contact within Home Instead/Honor's marketing function was identified. If Atkinson does not respond, the next-best option per the rules would be a Strategic Partnerships-type role, none of which were identified in current search results — this would need further research before a second outreach attempt.
