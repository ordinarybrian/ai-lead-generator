## Important fit distinction vs. a literal "senior center" directory

LeadingAge represents housing- and care-delivery organizations (life plan communities, nursing homes, affordable senior housing, home health/hospice, PACE) — not standalone community "senior centers" in the traditional sense (drop-in community/multipurpose centers offering meals, classes, and social programs for older adults). That segment is more squarely the territory of NCOA's National Institute of Senior Centers (see `leads/national-council-on-aging/organization.md`).

This means SeniorCenter.com's literal concept — a national directory of senior centers — is a **comparatively softer, more indirect fit** for LeadingAge than for an organization actually built around senior centers. LeadingAge's plausible use case is closer to a general public-facing aging-services resource or "find a community" tool than a literal senior-center finder.

## Potential SeniorCenter.com use (working hypothesis)

LeadingAge is fundamentally a B2B/association organization (member-facing, policy- and advocacy-oriented) without a large direct-to-consumer digital presence comparable to AARP or NCOA. SeniorCenter.com could plausibly work as:

- A consumer-facing front door that helps families discover LeadingAge member communities and aging services, extending LeadingAge's advocacy work into direct public visibility it doesn't currently have.
- A caregiver-resource or community-information hub loosely branded around LeadingAge's mission, without requiring the association to build literal senior-center listings.

This is a working strategic hypothesis, not a claim about LeadingAge's current plans or priorities.
