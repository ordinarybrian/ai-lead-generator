# Humana

## Organization

- Organization: Humana Inc.
- Website: https://www.humana.com/
- Organization type: Publicly traded (NYSE: HUM) health insurance company. Major Medicare Advantage insurer in the U.S., with CenterWell as its healthcare-delivery subsidiary (senior-focused primary care, pharmacy, home health).
- CEO: Jim (James) Rechtin — became President & CEO effective July 2024 (joined the company January 2024 as President & COO). Not the recommended contact per role-priority rules; noted for context only.
- Relevant audience/market: Medicare-eligible seniors (Humana's core membership base), their caregivers/family members, and the broader Medicare Advantage market. Humana explicitly markets senior-focused wellness, social, and community programming, not just insurance.

## Note: do not confuse with Humana AB (Sweden)

Search results for "Humana + Chief Marketing Officer" also surface **Humana AB**, a Swedish care-services company (appointed Gabriella Reuterswärd as CMO) — this is an unrelated company. All research below is specific to **Humana Inc.**, the U.S. health insurer at humana.com, which is the correct target given the lead-index entry (organization type: Healthcare/Medicare).

## Why Humana is a strong strategic fit for SeniorCenter.com

This is the strongest literal-match angle found across either organization researched in this batch:

- **Humana Neighborhood Centers** — Humana operates a network of physical "neighborhood centers" where members can attend supervised social, educational, and wellness activities, and the company runs an online search/directory tool at humana.com/humana-neighborhood-centers/search for members to find their local center and browse activities.
- **CenterWell Senior Primary Care** — Humana's CenterWell subsidiary operates 220+ senior-focused primary care centers (with plans for 30-50 new centers/year through 2025), many of which include community activity spaces open to local seniors (movie nights, bingo, Medicare/nutrition education classes), not just patients.
- **Points of Caregiving** — a Humana caregiver-support program aimed at the nation's family caregivers, alongside an existing "Caring for Others" resource hub at humana.com/caring-for-others.
- **SilverSneakers** — Humana also offers/promotes SilverSneakers fitness and social programming for members.

In short: Humana already operates the real-world thing SeniorCenter.com's positioning describes (a network of local senior centers with directory search, activities, and caregiver resources) — it is not a stretch to imagine SeniorCenter.com as a consumer-facing umbrella brand or directory front door for these programs, distinct from the insurance-sales-heavy humana.com main site.

This is a working strategic hypothesis, not a claim about Humana's current plans or budget priorities.
