# Caring.com

## Organization

- Organization: Caring.com (legal entity: Caring, LLC)
- Website: https://www.caring.com
- Organization type: Senior living/care referral directory and review platform, founded 2007.

## Ownership change (important, sourced)

**Caring.com was acquired by SilverAssist on January 12, 2026**, joining SilverAssist's existing portfolio alongside **Oasis Senior Advisors** (a national network of local Certified Senior Advisors) and **ElderLife Financial**. Per [Pulse2's coverage](https://pulse2.com/silverassist-acquires-caring-com-to-expand-national-senior-housing-referral-network/):

- SilverAssist's stated plan is to **integrate** Caring.com, not run it standalone — "pair Caring.com's scale and digital reach with a more localized, relationship-based advisory model through Oasis Senior Advisors, while layering in financing and affordability support via ElderLife Financial."
- Caring.com is explicitly positioned as **the digital-demand-generation layer** within this combined ecosystem.
- Quoted executives are SilverAssist's Greg Mason and Oasis Senior Advisors' John Benbrook — **not** anyone from Caring.com's own prior leadership.
- No source found states whether Caring.com's pre-acquisition executives (CEO Jim Rosenthal, Chief Growth Officer Cindy Starr) remain in their roles post-acquisition. Absence of a leadership mention in the announcement is not evidence either way.

Prior ownership: Caring.com was previously owned by a private equity group (acquisition completed ~May 2018, per Jim Rosenthal bio research); this January 2026 deal is a second ownership change.

## Strategic implications for this outreach (working analysis)

- **Cuts both ways.** SilverAssist is an active acquirer with a stated appetite for expanding "digital reach" — that could make them *more* receptive to a domain acquisition that fits the same thesis. But a company mid-integration (6 months in, as of 2026-07-22) often pauses new discretionary spend until the integration settles, which could make them *less* receptive right now.
- **Decision authority is genuinely unclear.** Cindy Starr's title (Chief Growth Officer) suggests she'd normally own this kind of decision, but post-acquisition, major digital-asset or M&A-adjacent decisions may now route through SilverAssist parent leadership (Mason/Benbrook), not Caring.com's own executives. Do not assume she has unilateral authority here.
- **Competitive/positioning note:** Caring.com already frames itself as a national digital discovery/referral layer — conceptually adjacent to what SeniorCenter.com could be. The pitch should acknowledge this is a complementary front door, not compete with their own positioning.

## Potential SeniorCenter.com use (working hypothesis)

Given the stated post-acquisition strategy (digital scale + local advisory + financing, combined), SeniorCenter.com could work as an additional top-of-funnel entry point feeding into that combined referral network — similar in spirit to the A Place for Mom angle, but the timing/authority uncertainty here is real and should shape how the email is framed (lower-key, more exploratory, acknowledging the acquisition directly).

This is a working hypothesis, not a claim about SilverAssist's or Caring.com's plans.
