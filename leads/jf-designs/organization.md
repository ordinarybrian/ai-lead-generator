## WordPress maintenance retainer — key finding: hosting is explicitly excluded

This was the central open question from the prior lead-index notes (whether Jay Forde competes with Inland Elite on hosting or could be an infrastructure-partner candidate). Per `/wordpress-maintenance/` (retrieved 2026-08-10):

- Plans "starting at $39/month." Exact tier names/prices beyond the starting figure were not published on the page.
- Included: core WordPress + plugin updates, periodic malware/performance scans, scheduled cloud backups (weekly or daily), uptime monitoring, minor changes/adjustments within 24 hours, and periodic reports.
- **Web hosting is explicitly not included.** The page states directly: *"I do not provide support for hosting-related issues directly."*
- No specific hosting provider is named or recommended anywhere on the page or elsewhere on the site.
- No migration service is mentioned on this page.

**This resolves the open question in Inland Elite's favor as a non-competing, complementary offering**: Jay Forde sells WordPress-level care (updates, backups, malware/uptime monitoring, minor edits) but explicitly disclaims hosting-level support. His clients still need somewhere to actually host their sites and someone to call when a hosting-layer problem occurs — a gap Inland Elite's hosting/infra support could fill behind his retainer without any overlap in what he already sells. This does not resolve whether he already refers clients to a specific host he trusts (no evidence found either way — worth asking directly rather than assuming a vacancy).

## Why this could be a fit (and why it's weaker than the CSV row implies)

- Positive: Solo WordPress-focused freelancer whose own maintenance offering has a clean, explicit boundary at the hosting layer — genuinely lower channel-conflict risk than most maintenance-selling freelancers, since he says outright he doesn't do hosting support.
- Negative / reassessment needed: He is not a North Idaho or Spokane-area local business by any evidence found — he's a Seattle-based solo operator running a nationwide, programmatic local-SEO landing-page strategy where North Idaho is one of many keyword-targeted markets, not a genuine community tie. PROJECT.md's target categories and positioning are built around *local* designers/agencies who can become real local referral relationships; that premise is not well supported here. This is a materially different situation from an actually-local freelancer like Kevin Raverty at 509 Designs (see that lead's files for contrast), who is genuinely Spokane-based.
- The "100% referral based, I don't advertise" positioning stated on his own About page sits in tension with the extensive programmatic SEO landing-page network found — worth treating his self-description with some skepticism rather than at face value.
