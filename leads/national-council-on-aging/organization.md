# National Council on Aging (NCOA)

## Organization

- Organization: National Council on Aging (NCOA)
- Website: https://www.ncoa.org/
- Organization type: National nonprofit focused on policy, programs, and benefits access to help older adults age well — a mission-driven advocacy and program organization, not a membership trade association for providers in the way LeadingAge is.
- President & CEO: Ramsey Alwin — verified 2026-08-06 via NCOA's own leadership page. She joined as President & CEO in August 2020; previously held VP, Economic Security at NCOA (2009–2015) and led financial-resilience work at AARP in between.

## National Institute of Senior Centers (NISC) — the key strategic fact

NCOA operates the **National Institute of Senior Centers (NISC)**, described (per a search snippet of NCOA's own NISC page, not independently fetched this session) as the largest national membership body dedicated to senior center professionals, with a network of 2,000+ affiliate members nationwide.

This is the single most important fact differentiating NCOA from LeadingAge for this project: NCOA is the organization that actually runs the closest thing the U.S. has to a national senior-center network. SeniorCenter.com's literal concept — a national destination for finding senior centers — maps almost exactly onto NISC's existing mission and membership base, rather than requiring NCOA to invent a new program area. This is a meaningfully stronger and more literal fit than at LeadingAge, whose membership is housing/care providers rather than standalone senior centers.

NCOA also already runs at least one large public-facing digital tool in a similar spirit — BenefitsCheckUp.org, a consumer-facing benefits-screening tool — establishing precedent that NCOA is comfortable owning and operating public-facing .org/.com-style digital properties as part of its mission delivery, not just policy/advocacy content.

## Potential SeniorCenter.com use (working hypothesis)

SeniorCenter.com could become the public-facing front door to NISC's senior center network — a searchable national directory that helps older adults and families find their local senior center, paired with NCOA's existing benefits, programs, and community-resource content. This is a working strategic hypothesis, not a claim about NCOA's current plans or roadmap.
