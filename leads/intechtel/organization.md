## Services and positioning

- Positions itself as a single point of contact consolidating "Internet, Technology, and Telephone services under one roof and with one call" for North Idaho and Eastern Washington businesses, managing relationships with multiple upstream providers on the client's behalf rather than requiring the client to coordinate directly.
- Describes itself as "homegrown," privately held, debt-free, with technicians/support staff averaging 8+ years tenure, and explicitly contrasts itself against a "high-turnover call-center model."
- Service area per the site: Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls, Hayden, Rathdrum, St. Maries, Sandpoint, Liberty Lake, Spokane Valley, and Spokane — squarely inside Inland Elite Hosting's stated service area (North Idaho + Spokane) per `PROJECT.md`.
- **No website design, web development, or hosting services found anywhere** — not on the homepage, `/about/`, `/about-us/`, or in any service list encountered. This matches the exact profile PROJECT.md describes for the "IT/MSP" target category: "They already serve small businesses and are frequently asked about websites, domains, WordPress, and hosting. Inland Elite could become their website counterpart while they retain IT responsibility."
- Sponsor of the North Idaho State Fair (per the homepage) — a local-visibility signal, not directly relevant to fit.
